Jaga-voda Idhayathil Lendhu Ezhudhina Vaazhkai Madal 🤍

En Chellam Pricila… 🥺🤍 Namma rendu peroda journey-a mudhal naal lendhu ippo varaikkum thirumbi paatha, ovvoru memory-um oru separate chapter maadhiri irukku. En life-la romba precious-aana oru person nee. May 26-la 7:40-ku oru simple "Hi" la aarambicha namma journey, appo namakku theriyama oru naal ivlo periya beautiful life story-a maarum nu. Appo nee enakku oru stranger, naan unakku oru stranger. Oru chinna message, konjam conversation, konjam curiosity, apram daily pesuradhu, random-a sirikkuradhu, chinna chinna vishayam share pannuradhu nu namma bond konjam konjama build aachu. Andha first message appo romba ordinary-ah therinjirukkalam; aana ippo thirumbi paatha, andha oru "Hi" dhaan en life-ku oru romba precious-aana soul-a kondu vandha first step. ❤️

Konjam konjam pesinom, konjam konjam purinjikittom, konjam konjam trust build pannom. Enakku theriyama nee en daily life-la oru part-a maarita. Morning-la edhavadhu nadandha un kitta sollanum nu thonum; happy-ah irundhaalum share panna thonum; kashtama irundhaalum un kitta pesanum nu thonum. Oru naal pesama irundhaalum edho missing maadhiri feel aaga aarambichadhu. Un presence enakku oru comfort-a maariduchu. Friendship nu aarambicha oru bond, namakku theriyama oru naal heart-ku romba close-aana relationship-a maariduchu. 🤍

Namma journey-la periya periya moments mattum illa dii; actually namma story-ku real beauty kuduthadhe namma kutty kutty moments dhaan. Oru "saaptiya?", oru "enga irukka?", oru random joke, oru cute fight, apram "seri pesu" nu marubadiyum normal aaguradhu — indha small things dhaan ippo enakku priceless memories. Nee sila neram poi kovapaduva, naan unna kalaaipen; aana rendu perukkum theriyum, andha kovam romba neram irukkaadhu nu. Namma perfect-ah irundhadhu nala namma story beautiful illa; imperfect moments-aiyum serndhu cross pannadhunaala dhaan namma story beautiful! ❤️

May 26 first page-na, September 27 en heart-la oru important chapter. Andha naal lendhu unna friend-ah paatha feeling maariduchu. Un character-a paatha pidichadhu, un heart-a purinjikitta pidichadhu, un smile-a paatha happiness vandhadhu. November 18-oda andha call en memory-la innum oru special page. Hours-ah pesinom, sirichom, namma life-la nadandhadhellam share pannom. Un kitta pesumbodhu naan comfortable-ah irundhen. January 12… manasula irundha feelings-a un kitta sollanum nu dhairiyam vandha unforgettable naal. Andha naal lendhu namma journey-ku vera oru meaning vandhuduchu.

August 9… un special naal. Un kitta pesa midnight 12 varaikkum wait pannina andha excitement, nee happy-ah irundha naanum happy aana feeling eppovume en memory-la irukkum! Namma first Pongal-um enakku romba special. Sweet Pongal maadhiri nee en life-la sweetness, warmth, happiness kondu vandha. 🌾🤍

Un 10th standard 372 mark pathi ninaikkumbodhu enakku eppovume proud feeling. Un hard work, dedication ellame paatha romba perumai. Nee life-la innum neraya heights reach pannanum dii! Un dreams achieve pannumbodhu adha celebrate panna vendiya person-ah naan iruppen!

Pricila pondati… na dhaan un purusha, nee dhaan en pondati! ❤️

Un mela promise-aa solluren — na drink panna matten, vera ponna paka matten, pessa matten, thappu panna matten! Unakku unmaiya irrupen dii! Life long unkuda mattum tha dii irrupen!
